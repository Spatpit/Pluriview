# Pluriview v0.7.6

Use your apps right from their tiles, record and screenshot your canvas, group
tiles into live layouts, and get started with a guided tour.

## Getting Started tour

The first time Pluriview opens, Mira, Pluriview's mascot, walks you through the
main features in eight short pages, each tip with a small looping animation and
your own shortcuts. Reopen it any time from **Help → Getting Started**.

## Use apps from their tiles

Double-click a window tile, or right-click it and choose **Interact**. Pluriview
zooms the tile to the window's real size and moves the app's own window over
it, so clicks and typing reach the app. Click outside it or **Done** to put the
window back where it was. **Show Original Window** in the tile menu does what
double-click used to do.

## Screenshots and recording

- **Shift+S** saves the selected tiles as one PNG, laid out as they sit on the
  canvas.
- **Shift+R** records the selected tiles to MP4 with sound, **P** pauses.
- The **Screenshots panel** (`S`) lists your shots and recordings in folders.
  Sort them into folders, send them to the Recycle Bin, or drag them back onto
  the canvas.

## Groups, Control Tile Center and Picture in Picture

- **Ctrl+G** groups tiles so they move, resize, pin and hide together. Arrange a
  group with one, two or three large tiles or all equal (`Alt+1`–`Alt+4`); it
  re-flows when tiles join or leave.
- The **Control Tile Center** (`C`) lists every tile as a card or on a map.
  Focus, hide, mute or move tiles from one place.
- **Picture in Picture** (`Shift+P`) pops tiles into a small window that stays
  above other apps and borderless games.

## Browser tiles

- New browser tiles have a toolbar and vertical tabs. Tabs can be dragged
  between tiles or out onto the canvas.
- `Shift`+scroll scrolls a page without entering it, tiles show unread counts,
  and **Fit to Video** crops a tile to the page's player.
- Sharper text on browser tiles you aren't using, and no more jumping when you
  start or stop using one.
- Microsoft Defender SmartScreen warnings are back on, crashed pages reload by
  themselves, and **Tools → Clear Browser Data...** signs you out everywhere.

## Interface

- New **Tools** menu, tidier **View**, **Workspace** and tile menus, and a
  searchable shortcut editor in **Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts**.
- Sharper text from the updated interface library.
- New window tiles take their window's shape instead of a fixed 4:3 box.

## Security and fixes

- Saved workspaces no longer keep passwords, one-time codes or share secrets
  found in web addresses.
- The video player library loads only from Pluriview's own folder, and stream
  addresses from Streamlink must be ordinary web addresses.
- A crafted GIF can no longer make Pluriview reserve gigabytes of memory, and
  very large images no longer close it.
- Fixed thin black bars on video tiles, stuck drags, and workspaces carrying
  selections or recordings over when switching.
- Less work while nothing moves, and `pluriview.exe` is about 6 MB smaller.

## Updating

Close Pluriview before replacing `pluriview.exe` and the included `lib` folder.
The ad blocker now lives in `lib` too. Keep your existing `pluriview_data`
folder to preserve workspaces, settings and browser data. Existing keyboard
shortcuts are preserved.

## Downloads

- **Full:** `Pluriview-v0.7.6-windows-x64-full.zip` — includes the video runtime
  for videos, folder playlists and video wallpapers.
- **Checksums:** `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Keep the included `lib` folder next to `pluriview.exe`. Browser tiles require
Microsoft WebView2 Runtime. Streamlink is an optional separate installation
for supported stream URLs opened in video tiles.
